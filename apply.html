Membership

Join the Collective

We're building a vetted network of nonprofits serving San Joaquin County — starting in Mountain House (95391) and growing across the 209. Membership is intentionally selective so it stays trustworthy for the community, local businesses, and the city.

Who can join right now

  • Physical address in ZIP 95391 (Mountain House) only, for now. We're starting narrow so we can test the site and vetting process properly — we plan to open registration to other San Joaquin County ZIP codes once that's done.
  • Registered 501(c)(3) nonprofits only (or fiscally sponsored charitable projects).
  • Organizations doing real, ongoing work in the community — not just registered on paper.
  • Willingness to join a short personal vetting call before admission.

What members get

  • A spot on the shared community calendar — list your own events any time, no separate approval needed once you're a member.
  • A collective voice when requesting things like facility access and city support.
  • Matching with local sponsors looking for a cause aligned with their values.
  • A public profile page with your logo, a banner photo, and your announcements.

There's no automatic upload here — the site has no backend. During your vetting call, send us your logo and a banner photo (a good event photo works great) and we'll add them to your profile for you.
